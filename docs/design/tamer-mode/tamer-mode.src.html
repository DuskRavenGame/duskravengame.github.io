Tamer Mode

The Beast Tamer stops being a class with a weapon and becomes a game of its own. You catch monsters in the towers and bond with one of them: in the tower it is you and your pet, and that pet is the character you raise. No blacksmith, no weapon: pets grow from experience and a currency of their own, and a permanent Taming Rank decides how strong a pet you can bond with. Teams come later, in a PvP mode of their own.

The loop

Climb→ Catch→ Raise→ Bond→ Climb deeper↺

Deeper floors hold rarer catches, and rarer pets need a higher Taming Rank, which comes from climbing. Each part pulls on the next.

Rarity, from one picture

Every catch rolls a rarity. A rare monster can never come out below Rare. The same Venom Drake below is the game's own picture with only a frame, a glow and a colour shift, so the whole ladder costs no new art.

Rarity is the chase inside a tier, so its steps are gentle: a Mythic is twice a Common of the same kind. What tower a pet comes from matters more (see "Later pets are stronger"). Numbers and odds are first guesses.

One bonded pet

In the tower it is the tamer and one pet, as the fight already shows two fighters facing each other. The rest of what you catch waits in the den. The bonded pet is the character: its level, its skills, its rarity are what you raise over weeks.

The tamer commands, the pet fights

The tamer never attacks. Everything you level and buy on the tamer feeds the pet: the tamer has three path stats, and each pet grows from one of them. (Today's staff blow does 0 to 1% of the Tamer's damage in the bot's probe, so nothing is lost.)

Tamer stat · Might

Physical

Hits and bites: crushing, slashing, piercing. Big single blows, stuns, armor breaking.

Tamer stat · Arcana

Magic

Spells the pet casts: fire, ice, lightning, arcane, holy. Goes through armor, hits a monster's weakness.

Tamer stat · Instinct

Primal

Affliction, the game's damage over time: bleed and poison stacks, and critical strikes.

The third path deals damage on purpose. The map records that every choice which only kept the hero alive was a trap, 10 to 37 floors behind its neighbours, because a climb stops at the fight's clock and only damage beats the clock. So staying alive is a passive any pet can have, never a whole path.

The starters, one a path: the Bear (Physical), the Wolf, whose bites bleed (Primal), and the Hawk as a storm hawk diving with lightning (Magic; "Storm Hawk" is already one of its perks).

How each path fights

Physical

Full hits every turn. Its skill comes back every 6 turns. Might raises every hit, so most of its damage is the plain attack.

Magic

Weak hits, 40% of a physical pet's. Its spell comes back every 3 turns, and Arcana raises spell damage and makes spells come back faster. Every magic passive changes how its spells work, so a magic pet lives by its spells.

Primal

Lighter hits that stack bleed or poison, or land critical hits. Its skill comes back every 5 turns. Instinct raises stacks and critical hits, so its damage grows over a fight.

Slow spells never punish

An ordinary fight lasts two or three turns, so a pet that waits for its spell could lose it to bad timing. Four rules stop that.

Rule

The wait carries over

A spell's cooldown runs across fights, as abilities already do today, and is cleared only when a new run starts. A fight that ends early never wastes the charge.

Rule

Nothing is overkill

Spell damage beyond what kills a monster lands on the next one. A big spell on a nearly dead monster is never wasted, and a spell landing over two turns finishes on whoever is there.

Rule

A longer wait pays more

Every spell is worth at least one turn of damage for each turn it takes to come back. Over a whole cycle a magic pet deals what a physical one does: 40% hits plus a 3-turn spell every 3 turns is 1.4 turns a turn, against about 1.4 for a physical pet. The script that builds this page refuses a spell worth less than its wait.

Rule

No dead turns

No pet ever skips a turn to recover: the Iron Golem's rest is gone, and its big spell simply takes a turn longer to come back. What builds up between spells (the Golem's charge, the Magma Hound's heat) is kept between fights for the whole run.

The petcheck proves it: a magic pet must win an ordinary fight in no more turns than a physical pet of the same tier, give or take 10%, and less than 10% of all spell damage may go unused.

Later pets are stronger

Every pet has a tier: the tower it comes from, in badge order. Each tier is x1.5 the one before, and a boss tame sits half a tier above its tower. So moving on pays: a Common from tier 6 (x7.6) beats a Mythic from tier 3 (x2.25, doubled to x4.5).

  • A new pet is caught at a level near the floor you catch it on, so moving on never means raising a pet from level 1 again.
  • Awakening, for a favourite: Essence and a trophy from the next tower's boss raise a pet one tier. Dear on purpose, so moving on is usually cheaper, but a loved pet never has to be left behind.

Every pet has a kit

All 72 monsters and bosses in the towers, each with one skill (used on a cooldown, like the abilities today) and one passive (always on). Every kit comes from the monster's own line in towers.js, so each pet plays the way it fought you. Kits are built from shared pieces (turns of damage, a lost turn, poison or bleed stacks, a share through armor, healing), so 72 pets are 72 lines of data rather than 72 pieces of code. Rarity strengthens the passive, and a Legendary gains a second one.

Making every pet viable

Tower

A niche each

  • Matchups: towers resist some damage types, so another path suits another tower.
  • Jobs: boss killer, room clearer, deep-floor survivor, farmer.
  • Farming passives (gold, Essence, catch chance) make a weaker fighter the right pick for a farming run.

Viable means "the best pet for something", not "as deep as every other pet".

Proof

The petcheck

  • A new bot test like the perk check: each pet climbs a tower, and the depths are compared.
  • Each pet must be the best, or near it, at its niche: its matchup tower, bosses, gold per hour.
  • For PvP: every pet fights every other at equal level, and no pet wins less than 40% or more than 60%.

This turns "every choice is viable" into numbers checked before anything ships, the way perks are checked today.

The ideas

Core

Catching

  • Every monster you beat has a small chance to join you.
  • Rare monsters have a much better chance and roll higher rarities.
  • Catching goes on while you are away, with a "caught while away" list.
  • Snares (bought with the new currency) raise the chance for the next fight.

Generous early, so the first catch comes in minutes. Then slower, and rarity carries the chase.

Core

Raising pets

  • Your bonded pet earns the experience of every fight.
  • Beast Essence, the new currency, trains pets in the den so they are not left behind.
  • Releasing a pet you do not want pays Essence, more for rarer ones.
  • Each pet keeps its own level, so changing your bond is a real choice.

One new currency, Essence, for pets. Totems stay as they are: what the Tamer earns for the other classes.

Core

Taming Rank, like gym badges

  • A permanent rank that survives ascending and becoming a legend.
  • Win a badge by beating a tower's great boss with the Tamer. Eight towers, eight badges.
  • Each badge opens the next tier of pets: tier 2 needs one badge, tier 8 needs seven.

Better than a Tamer-level requirement. The Tamer's level resets when it ascends, so a level-locked pet would be taken from you every ascension. A rank never takes a pet away.

Core

Hunting a kind on purpose

  • Pick a kind to track: it turns up more often in its tower.
  • The bestiary's ranks (10, 100, 1,000 kills) raise its catch chance.
  • Lures draw one kind for the next run.

Cheap to build: the bestiary already counts kills for every kind of monster.

Later

Boss tames

  • Bosses are never caught in a normal fight.
  • After enough kills of a boss, a Bond Trial opens: a harder fight that ends in a catch.
  • A boss pet brings its own mechanic: shield, summons, enrage, reflect.

The prize of the mode. The boss mechanics already exist in game/bosses.js, so their pets reuse them.

Later

Climbing rarity

  • Three of one kind and rarity merge into one of the next rarity.
  • Mythic only comes from merging.
  • Stars on a pet for each duplicate fed to it.

Gives every duplicate a use, which a catching game needs badly.

Later

New pets, new places

  • Pet-only monsters, caught only in the Wild Reaches.
  • Rare colour variants with their own picture.
  • New art made the way the tower backgrounds were made.

The 72 pictures we have are enough to launch. New art comes after the loop is fun.

Core

Why keep catching

  • Matchups: towers resist some kinds, so another pet suits another tower.
  • Feeding: duplicates merge into your bonded pet for rarity and stars.
  • The chase: rare, boss and Mythic pets.
  • Your PvP team, later, comes from the den.

This is what keeps one pet in the tower from making the rest of the collection pointless.

Later

PvP teams

  • A mode of its own, apart from the tower.
  • Pick a team of three from your den and save it.
  • A friend's saved team fights yours by itself, so nobody needs to be online at once.

The accounts and friends we have on Supabase are enough for a first version. Teams stay out of the tower, so the tower stays simple.

Your call

What starting over does

  • Option A: pets keep everything when the Tamer ascends.
  • Option B: pets keep rarity and stars, but levels drop back.
  • Option C: like B, but the rank sets a floor the levels never fall below.

C. Ascending still means something, and nothing you caught ever feels lost.

1

Pets replace the weapon

built, not live

The tamer stops attacking and gets the three path stats. The wolf, bear and hawk become three starter pets, one a path, each with its skill and passive, levels and experience, and you bond with one. The blacksmith is closed to the Tamer, and an old Tamer save bonds with the companion it was using.

Built 17 October: the Pets card (in place of the blacksmith), Might, Arcana and Instinct, the starter kits (Bear: Crushing Maul and Guardian; Wolf: Rend and bleeding Pack Tactics; Hawk: Storm Dive and Storm Eye), pet levels from kills, Essence training, half levels kept on ascending, and the save step. Then the hawk's lag was traced to the sweep, not its power: its 3-turn spell kills ended the sweep early, so after each death it restarted 200 floors low. Fixed (a magic pet's fight won by its first spell counts as quick), then tuned: Arcana's haste capped at every other turn, Arcana no longer gives critical damage, Storm Dive 2 dives, the hawk's old "harry" removed, wolf bites +28%. In 100 bot hours: hawk 579, bear 534, wolf 520.

Catching and the collection

built, not live

Catch chance on every win, rarity rolls, the collection grid with the rarity frames, releasing for Essence, catches while away. Launch with about a dozen catchable pets, three a path plus a farmer or two, each with its kit, and the petcheck in the bot.

Built 17 October: every kit is a block of data in classes/pets.js, made of shared pieces (turns of damage, a spell, stacks, a stun, a guard, a heal...) and a passive of bonus words. All 72 monsters and bosses have one, adapted from the table below where the game had no such rule yet. A caught pet walks its path: its plain attack and its perks are its path starter's, its skill and passive its own. 1% catch on a win, ten times for a rare monster, rarity rolled; a second catch is the better rarity or Essence; a new pet starts at three quarters of the bonded pet's level; catches while away are in the report. The Bond with grid shows the pets in their rarity's frame. The bot's allpets and pets= climb with each pet.

To decide together

Starting over

Option A, B or C above. I lean C.

One currency or two

Essence for pets, totems for the other classes. Or fold everything into totems. I lean two.

Changing your bond

Free at any time, free between runs, or at a cost? I lean free between runs, so matchups matter without constant swapping.

If your pet falls

Does the run end with it, as when the hero falls, or can the tamer fight on alone for a while?

Rank or Tamer level

What gates a strong pet. I lean rank, since the level resets.

What other classes get

Only totems, as now? Or could a pet be lent to another class for a run?

Catch odds

How soon the first catch, and how rare a Legendary should feel.

Moving path points

Can Might, Arcana and Instinct points be moved freely when you bond a pet of another path? I lean free, or swapping pets costs a whole climb.

Risks

  • Every class is built around a weapon: the blacksmith, the boss weapon, keystones, perks and abilities picked by weapon. The Tamer needs a pet-shaped answer to each.
  • The balance bot plays weapons. It has to learn to catch and raise pets, or the mode cannot be measured.
  • Friends play the live game, so every old Tamer save needs a clean step into the new mode.
  • A catching game lives on a stream of new pets. New art is the slowest part, so the loop has to be fun with the pictures we already have.
